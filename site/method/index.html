Method and assumptions

Recessed light count planner equation

Minimum fixtures = ceiling((room area × target illuminance) / (fixture lumens × utilization)); Columns = ceiling(sqrt(N × length / width)); Rows = ceiling(N / columns); Spacing = dimension / count.

Step-by-step logic

Multiply room floor area by target illuminance (foot-candles in US units, lux in metric) to calculate lumens required at the working plane. Divide by per-fixture lumens times the utilization fraction and round up. Fit a balanced rectangular grid covering at least that minimum count and report center-to-center spacing.

Units and rounding

Lengths convert at 0.3048 m per foot; 1 foot-candle equals exactly 10.7639104 lux (1 lumen per square foot). Fixture, row, and column counts round up to whole integers.

Limits and edge cases

Target values are planning guides, not building-code certifications. Beam angle, ceiling height, glare, cabinet overhangs, joist obstructions, IC/damp ratings, and circuit loads must be verified against fixture datasheets and a qualified electrician.
